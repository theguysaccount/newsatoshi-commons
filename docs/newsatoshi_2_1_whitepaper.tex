\begin{abstract}
Bitcoin~\cite{nakamoto2008} turned the ordering of electronic payments into a cryptographic proof, yet left validity to unbounded full-history re-execution, relied on elliptic-curve signatures vulnerable to Shor's algorithm, and left recursive spending covenants unresolved. \emph{A Store of Order} (\url{https://newsatoshi.org/white-paper/}) reduces private electronic cash and validity proofs to a single hash function (\texttt{SHA-256}) merge-mined with Bitcoin (\texttt{AuxPoW chainid = 20051}).

Two 2026 milestones reshaped formal cryptography: the June 2026 Orchard circuit vulnerability established machine-checked balance proofs as mandatory for private money, while the November 2026 release of a 17-million-line AI-assisted Lean~4 proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis---and rapid independent audits by mathematicians and frontier reasoning models---highlighted that \emph{proof compilation alone does not guarantee human auditability}. For monetary consensus, specification kernels must remain small enough for exhaustive line-by-line inspection.

We present \textbf{NEWSATOSHI/2.1}, a backwards-compatible soft-fork extension (\texttt{OP\_VERIFY\_COVENANT\_STARK}, \texttt{0xb4} redefining \texttt{OP\_NOP5}), accompanied by a \textbf{355-line, zero-dependency, zero-\texttt{sorry} Lean~4.36.0 soundness kernel} (\texttt{formal/NewsatoshiCovenants.lean}) proving nine theorems over Winterfell~0.13's exact prime field $p = 2^{128} - 45\cdot 2^{40} + 1$. We prove injective 3-limb SHA-256 digest packing, zero modular wrap-around in 63-bit bit-decomposition range checks, exact integer value conservation, exact Euclidean basis-point floor uniqueness, unbounded inductive covenant continuity, monotonic Web4 child-agent spending bounds, and cross-chain HTLC anti-race safety. We further prove a Budish rented-hashpower contagion-isolation proposition, report live Sourcify-verified deployments of our ownerless SHA-256 HTLC across four EVM mainnets (\texttt{0xF564A8F600B02981Bc02aC62a2B7d0C8dC638987}), and formulate three open cryptographic conjectures.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: The Tiny-Kernel Auditability Threshold}
As AI reasoning systems begin generating and auditing multi-million-line Lean formalizations, formal verification transitions from an academic luxury to a core primitive of protocol engineering. However, monetary consensus differs fundamentally from exploratory pure mathematics: a subtle definitional circularity or field-aliasing loophole inside a private-currency arithmetic circuit enables undetectable counterfeit inflation.

We adopt the \textbf{Tiny Verifier Kernel Principle}: every arithmetic and inductive invariant governing covenanted \texttt{LOVE} spends is specified in $355$ lines of stock Lean~4.36.0 with zero external library dependencies (\texttt{0} \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{0} \texttt{Classical.choice}), mirroring our executing Winterfell~0.13 STARK AIR (\texttt{src/stark.rs}) byte-for-byte.

\section{Nine Machine-Checked Soundness Theorems (Lean 4.36.0)}
Every theorem below compiles under stock \texttt{lean formal/NewsatoshiCovenants.lean} in $\approx 1.0\,\text{s}$ CPU time using only Lean's constructive core axioms (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}, or zero axioms):

\begin{theorem}[Injective 3-Limb Digest Packing]
Let $d = (l_0, l_1, l_2) \in [0,2^{88}) \times [0,2^{88}) \times [0,2^{80})$ represent a 256-bit SHA-256 digest packed into $\mathbb{F}_p^3$ via $(l_0 \bmod p, l_1 \bmod p, l_2 \bmod p)$. Because $2^{88} < p$, the packing map is strictly injective.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Algebraic Booleanity Over Any Prime Field]
For any prime $p$ and canonical $b < p$, $p \mid b(b-1) \implies b \in \{0, 1\}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[63-Bit Bit-Decomposition Soundness]
Given $a_0 = 0$, $a_{k+1} = 2a_k + b_k$, and $b_k \in \{0,1\}$ for $0 \le k < 63$, we have $a_{63} \le 2^{63}-1 < p$, ruling out negative-slack modular wrap-around ($p-1$) attacks.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Exact Integer Value Conservation]
For $\mathrm{input}, \mathrm{payout}, \mathrm{change} \le 2^{63}-1$, if $\mathrm{input} \equiv \mathrm{payout} + \mathrm{change} \pmod p$, then $\mathrm{payout} + \mathrm{change} = \mathrm{input}$ in $\mathbb{N}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[End-to-End Modular Basis-Point Floor Uniqueness]
For $\mathrm{input}, \mathrm{change}, r, u \le 2^{63}-1$ and $\mathrm{bps} \le 10000$, the modular constraints $\mathrm{input}\cdot\mathrm{bps} \equiv 10000\cdot\mathrm{change} + r \pmod p$ and $r + u \equiv 9999 \pmod p$ uniquely force $\mathrm{change} = \lfloor (\mathrm{input}\cdot\mathrm{bps})/10000 \rfloor$ in $\mathbb{N}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Inductive Recursive Covenant Continuity]
Across any valid sequence of $T \ge 0$ covenant transitions preserving $\mathrm{ruleHash}_{t+1} = \mathrm{ruleHash}_t$, $\mathrm{payout}_t + \mathrm{bal}_{t+1} = \mathrm{bal}_t$, and $\mathrm{payout}_t \le B_{\max}$, we have $\mathrm{ruleHash}_T = \mathrm{ruleHash}_0$, $\sum \mathrm{payout}_t + \mathrm{bal}_T = \mathrm{bal}_0$, and $\sum \mathrm{payout}_t \le T \cdot B_{\max}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Monotonic Web4 Child-Agent Spending Bounds]
For any parent-to-child autonomous agent replication chain of arbitrary depth $d$ enforcing $\mathrm{constHash}_{\text{child}} = \mathrm{constHash}_{\text{parent}}$, $\mathrm{maxSpend}_{\text{child}} \le \mathrm{maxSpend}_{\text{parent}}$, and $\mathrm{backingBps}_{\text{child}} \ge \mathrm{backingBps}_{\text{parent}}$, no descendant can widen spending authority or weaken reserve backing (proven with \textbf{zero axioms}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Cross-Chain SHA-256 HTLC Anti-Race Safety]
If $t_{\text{first}} \ge t_{\text{second}} + 120$ and the counterparty claims leg 2 at $t_{\text{claim}} < t_{\text{second}}$, then $t_{\text{claim}} + 120 < t_{\text{first}}$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Economic Security: Budish Contagion Isolation}
\begin{proposition}[L1 Blindness Against Rented-Hashpower \& Wash-Farming]
Under Budish's~\cite{budish2018} majority-attack inequality $P_{\text{attack}} < C_{\text{rent}}(z)$, coupling base-layer issuance to physical oracle attestations permits zero-marginal-cost wash-farming and spot-hashpower reorgs. \end{proposition}
